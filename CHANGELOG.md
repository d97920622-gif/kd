Every change to KD SOLUTION IT is recorded in this file. The entries are
generated from the commit history by `npm run changelog` and the file is
never edited by hand: a change that is not in the history is not in this
file, and a change that is committed appears here at the next handoff.

The format follows Keep a Changelog. Changes are grouped by the commit type
that introduced them and the newest entry is first within a section.
